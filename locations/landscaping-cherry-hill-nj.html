Local Landscaping Tips for Cherry Hill Homeowners

Cherry Hill sits in the heart of Camden County, where the soil tends to run heavy with clay — especially in older established neighborhoods. Clay soil drains poorly and compacts easily underfoot, which means aeration is one of the most impactful things you can do for a Voorhees lawn. We recommend aerating every fall, overseeding with a drought-tolerant fescue blend, and adding a layer of compost to help break up compaction over time. For properties near wooded areas, staying on top of leaf removal in late October and November is critical — wet, matted leaves are one of the leading causes of lawn disease and bare patches come spring.

Tip from our crew: Cherry Hill properties near Cooper River tend to see standing water after heavy rain. If you notice soggy spots that don't dry out within 24 hours, ask us about grading and drainage solutions — it can make a big difference in overall lawn health.
