Local Landscaping Tips for Haddonfield Homeowners

Haddonfield's tree canopy is one of its defining features, but all that shade creates real challenges for lawn care. Many Haddonfield yards struggle with thin, patchy grass under large oaks and maples. Rather than fighting the shade, we often recommend transitioning those areas to shade-tolerant ground covers or strategic mulching around tree bases — both of which look excellent and require far less maintenance. If you do want to maintain turf in shaded areas, a fine fescue blend is your best bet in South Jersey's climate. Also, Haddonfield's leaf fall is significant in October and November; a two-pass removal schedule (early and late fall) keeps lawns healthy going into winter.

Tip from our crew: Something we see often in Haddonfield: overgrown tree roots lifting sidewalks and hardscapes near property lines. While we can't handle root removal, we're happy to work around root systems with careful mulching and bed design that protects your trees and keeps your yard looking polished.
